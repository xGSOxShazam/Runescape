I

Dress for the skill

5–6% XP

Most full XP outfits give 5–6%. Elite sets often inherit an owned basic set’s bonus; they don’t add a second full bonus.

Explore XP outfits ↗
II

A little wisdom

4% XP

Wise 4 boosts eligible XP while equipped. Its 50,000 bonus XP daily cap is shared across skills. Protect your useful tool slots.

Understand Wise ↗
III

Light the incense

2% XP

Torstol incense reaches 2% at maximum potency. Check the cost and eligible activity before making it a daily habit.

Check the mechanics ↗
IV

Don’t forget your urns

25% URN BOOST

An enhancer boosts the urn’s reward by 25%, not all training XP. Urn types and activation levels vary by skill.

Get the maths right ↗

Stack smarter. XP bonuses, resource savings and faster actions are different benefits. They don’t all multiply together.

NOVEMBER DISPATCH02 NOV 2026

A new chapter
for training supplies.

Double XP runs November 6–16 with 48 usable hours. Many legacy XP supplies are scheduled to leave on November 23, including portables, pulse and cinder cores, proteans and silverhawks.

Use the stock you already own thoughtfully. Treat these benefits as temporary, rather than building a permanent loadout around them.
